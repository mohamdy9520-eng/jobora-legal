Jobora

A job search app that keeps your applications, interviews, and CVs in one place, so you always know where you stand.

What you can do with Jobora

Track every application
Record the company, the role, and the status of each application, and add your own notes.
Never miss an interview
Keep interview dates in one place and turn on optional reminders.
Keep your CVs organized
Store your CVs and cover letters alongside the applications they belong to.
Get AI-assisted suggestions
Optional AI features, such as CV suggestions, help you improve how you apply.
See your statistics
Clear numbers show how your job search is going, with optional weekly summaries.
Keep your account secure
Sign in with email or Google. Your data stays tied to your own account.
Go further with Jobora Pro
An optional premium subscription, purchased through Google Play.
